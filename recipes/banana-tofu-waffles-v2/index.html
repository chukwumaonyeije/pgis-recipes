v2 Changes & Rationale
What Changed from v1 — and Why

Two deliberate upgrades

1
Seltzer folded in last
1.2 cups cold sparkling water replaces 0.5 cups of plant milk (total milk down from 2 to 1.5 cups). Seltzer is never blended — carbonation is the whole mechanism. Blend it and the bubbles die, so it folds in right before cooking and the batter goes straight onto the iron.
→ Batter looses and aerates. Cook promptly.
2
2 scoops protein powder
Across 10 waffles, 1 scoop barely moves the per-waffle number. Two scoops adds a meaningful ~3–4g per waffle. Use unsweetened, no-stevia-flooded plant protein to stay protocol-clean. Protein powder makes batter thirstier — the seltzer fold-in compensates if it firms up too much.
→ ~12–14g protein per waffle vs. ~9–11g in v1

Ingredients · Dry Base
Dry Base

Dry Base

  • 3 cups oat flour — beta-glucan, glucose dampening
  • 1.2 cups chickpea flour — legume protein anchor, low GI
  • 2.4 scoops plant protein — pea or soy, unsweetened, no stevia
  • 2.4 tbsp baking powder — break up any protein clumps when mixing
  • 2.4 tsp cinnamon
  • 0.6 tsp salt
  • 0.6 cups walnuts, chopped — optional; omega-3, fat-buffering for glucose curve

Protein powder selection

Choose unsweetened pea or soy protein — no stevia, no flavoring agents that would push the recipe out of MD protocol. Break up clumps when whisking dry ingredients. Protein powder makes batter thirstier and can dry the waffle — don't overcook past crisp.

Batter hydration note
Oat + chickpea + protein flours all absorb aggressively. Rest the batter so they hydrate and thicken fully before seltzer goes in. If it firms up too much, the seltzer fold-in loosens it back.

Execution Protocol · Steps 1–5
Execution Protocol

Method — Steps 1–5

1
Flax eggs
Stir 3.6 tbsp flaxseed + 10.8 tbsp water. Rest until thick and gel-like. Do not skip rest time.
2
Press tofu
Press 16.8 oz tofu firmly while flax sets. Good pressing keeps batter from going watery — no oil needed to compensate.
3
Blend wet base
Blend pressed tofu, bananas, plant milk, vanilla, and flax eggs until completely smooth. No tofu lumps. No seltzer yet.
4
Mix dry
Whisk oat flour, chickpea flour, protein powder, baking powder, cinnamon, salt. Break up protein clumps now.
5
Combine + rest
Fold wet into dry. Stir in walnuts if using. Let rest — flours absorb and batter thickens.
PGIS Recipes 6 / 9
Execution Protocol · Steps 6–10
Steps 6–10

Finish & Cook

6
Preheat iron
Heat iron fully before seltzer goes in. Get it hot first — seltzer lift must carry through to cooking immediately.
7
Fold in seltzer
Gently fold 1.2 cups cold seltzer into rested batter. Fold, don't whisk. Batter loosens and looks airy. Cook promptly.
8
Cook waffles
Scoop onto hot iron. Cook longer than standard. Oat–chickpea–protein batter needs extra time to firm and crisp. Resist opening early.
9
Cool fully
Cool on wire rack so steam escapes. Prevents sogginess in the freezer.
10
Meal prep
Freeze flat on tray, then bag with parchment between. Reheat from frozen in toaster or oven until crisp.
The Seltzer Rule
Seltzer folds in LAST, right before cooking. Batter goes straight onto the iron.
Carbonation is the lift mechanism. Blend it and you knock the bubbles out. Don't let the batter sit once seltzer is in — cook promptly so the lift carries through.

PGIS Notes
PGIS Notes

Practitioner Notes

  • Chickpea flour cook time: Chickpea flour still needs full cook time to lose the raw-bean taste. Don't pull waffles early — the cook time is longer than a standard waffle by design.
  • Protein powder texture: Protein powder makes batter thirstier and can dry the waffle if overcooked. Stop at crisp, not hard. The seltzer fold-in corrects batter that firmed too much while resting.
  • Tofu pressing: The reason waffles don't need oil is pressed tofu — the structural dryness that oil usually provides. Under-pressed tofu = watery batter. Press firmly.
  • Freezer performance: Freezes well; reheats crispy in toaster or oven from frozen. Cool fully on rack before freezing to prevent sogginess.
  • CGM experiment: Higher protein should flatten the curve vs. v1. Tag the first batch with a note and compare against your prior log. Pair with berries + nuts or tahini.
  • Protocol fit: No added sugar, no oil. Banana sweetens. Tofu + chickpea + oat + protein = MD-compatible macrostructure.
