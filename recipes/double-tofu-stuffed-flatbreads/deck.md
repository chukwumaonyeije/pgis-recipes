# Double Tofu Stuffed Flatbreads <span class="tag">Featured</span>

<p class="lead">A savory, Szechuan-leaning comfort-meal deck built around silken-tofu dough, super-firm tofu filling, and a portion-aware PGIS strategy that keeps protein central even when the meal feels more indulgent.</p>

<div class="card">

**Category:** Main Dish / Savory Meal / Recovery Meal  
**Glycemic impact:** Moderate to moderately high; softened by protein but still denser than lighter PGIS defaults  
**Training context:** Post-run, post-strength, planned weekend batch prep, or shared-meal settings  
**Protocol:** PGIS savory comfort-meal strategy with portion awareness

</div>

<div class="split-note">
  <span>PGIS Recipes</span>
  <span>Yield: 6 stuffed flatbreads</span>
</div>

---

## Overview

- Uses tofu twice: silken tofu gives the dough structure and super-firm tofu anchors the filling.
- Lands closer to a comfort-food main than a low-fat PGIS bowl, but still performs better than plain stuffed bread.
- Strongest use case is deliberate meal planning, not casual grazing.
- Best PGIS move is to treat one flatbread as the center of a plate rather than the whole plate.

---

## Ingredient Set
### Dough + garnish

- 350 g plain flour
- 300 g silken tofu
- Pinch of salt
- Garnish: spring onions, sesame seeds, chili oil

<div class="card">
The dough is unusually protein-supportive for a flatbread because the silken tofu does real structural work rather than acting like a minor add-in.
</div>

---

## Ingredient Set
### Filling build

- 400 g super-firm tofu
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 inch ginger
- 3 spring onions
- 1.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp doubanjiang
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1.5 tbsp rice vinegar
- Sesame oil
- Vegan cheese

<div class="card">
The doubanjiang, soy, ginger, and cheese make the filling intensely satisfying, which is exactly why portion strategy matters.
</div>

---

## Execution Protocol
### Dough and filling

1. **Mix and knead** flour, silken tofu, and salt until smooth and only lightly tacky.
2. **Rest** the dough under a damp towel for 30 minutes.
3. **Build aromatics** by frying garlic, ginger, and white spring-onion parts in a splash of sesame oil.
4. **Crumble and cook** the super-firm tofu long enough to drive off excess moisture.
5. **Sauce the filling** with brown sugar, doubanjiang, soy sauce, and rice vinegar, then cool fully.

---

## Shape and Cook <span class="tag">Key Step</span>

1. **Divide** rested dough into 6 balls and roll each one thinly.
2. **Fill** each round with one-sixth of the tofu mixture and a generous handful of grated vegan cheese.
3. **Seal** by bringing edges together, pinching closed, flipping seam-side down, and gently flattening again.
4. **Pan-cook** in a dry non-stick skillet over medium heat, about 3 minutes per side with the lid on.
5. **Finish** with chili oil, green spring onions, and sesame seeds.

<div class="card">
The original source referenced a shaping video, so a first test flatbread is worth treating as calibration.
</div>

## PGIS Logic

- **Protein support:** Both dough and filling contribute tofu, which makes the meal more useful than ordinary stuffed flatbread.
- **Energy density watch:** Flour, vegan cheese, and finishing oil increase cost relative to lighter PGIS defaults.
- **Sodium awareness:** Doubanjiang, soy sauce, and cheese can stack quickly.
- **Adherence advantage:** Handheld savory meals can preserve consistency when bowls and scrambles start feeling repetitive.

<div class="card">
This is one of those recipes where strategic use matters more than pretending it is ultra-light.
</div>

---

## Best Use Pattern

- **Post-training meal:** 1 flatbread plus greens, slaw, or a lighter vegetable side.
- **Weekend prep:** Make the full batch and reheat in a dry skillet for better texture.
- **Shared table meal:** Useful when a strict low-fat plate would be harder to sustain socially.
- **Avoid the trap:** Eating several pieces without a vegetable side turns this from structured meal to energy-dense snack cycle.

---

## CGM Strategy

- Expect a higher response than lower-fat PGIS bowls, scrambles, or waffles.
- The tofu structure still improves the meal relative to standard cheese-stuffed breads.
- Best flattening move: keep the serving to 1 flatbread and pair it with high-volume vegetables.
- First adjustments if the curve runs high: reduce cheese load, lighten finishing oil, and tighten accompaniments before dropping the recipe.

<div class="card">
This is a better “planned indulgence” than an everyday baseline. That framing keeps it useful inside PGIS instead of working against it.
</div>

---

## Practical Notes

- Let the filling cool before shaping so the dough seals more cleanly.
- Use enough flour to roll thin without tearing, but do not over-dry the dough.
- Reheat in a dry pan instead of a microwave if crust texture matters.
- Source caveat: this deck was adapted from a social-post capture, not a formal recipe document.

<div class="card">
The win here is not making comfort food disappear. The win is making a savory comfort-style meal more structured, more protein-aware, and easier to use intentionally.
</div>
