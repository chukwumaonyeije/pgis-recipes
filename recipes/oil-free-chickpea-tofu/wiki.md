# Mastering Diabetes-Friendly Chickpea Tofu

This firm, sliceable chickpea tofu is an oil-free alternative to soy tofu. It is prepared from chickpea flour, water, lemon juice, and spices, making it naturally vegan, gluten-free, and low in fat. The recipe reflects the Mastering Diabetes emphasis on legumes, vegetables, herbs, spices, and other low-fat plant foods.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chickpea flour (nutrition estimate assumes 120 g; actual cup weight varies)
- 3 cups water, divided
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
- ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon salt, optional
- 1 pinch cayenne pepper or Nigerian red pepper, optional
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice

## Equipment

- Medium saucepan
- Whisk
- Heat-resistant spoon or silicone spatula
- 8-inch square pan or loaf pan
- Parchment paper

## Preparation

1. Line an 8-inch square pan or loaf pan with parchment paper. No oil is needed.
2. Combine the chickpea flour with **1½ cups cold water**. Whisk until completely smooth and free of lumps.
3. Add the remaining **1½ cups water**, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, turmeric, black pepper, salt, and optional cayenne to a saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil.
4. Slowly pour the chickpea-flour mixture into the saucepan while whisking continuously.
5. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Cook for 7–10 minutes, stirring continuously, until the mixture becomes very thick, glossy, and begins pulling away from the sides of the saucepan.
6. Stir in the lemon juice.
7. Immediately transfer the mixture to the prepared pan. Smooth the surface with a wet spatula.
8. Let it cool at room temperature for approximately 20 minutes.
9. Refrigerate for at least 1–2 hours, or until completely firm.
10. Lift the tofu from the pan and cut it into cubes, strips, or slabs.

## Oil-Free Crisping Methods

### Air fryer

Air-fry at 400°F for 15–18 minutes, turning halfway through cooking.

### Conventional oven

Arrange on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 425°F for 25–30 minutes, turning once.

### Stovetop

Brown the pieces in a high-quality nonstick skillet. Add small splashes of vegetable broth or water if needed to prevent sticking.

## Oil-Free Roasted Red-Pepper Sauce

### Ingredients

- 1 roasted red bell pepper
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove
- Smoked paprika, black pepper, and cayenne to taste

### Instructions

Blend all ingredients until smooth. Serve chilled or gently warmed.

## Mastering Diabetes-Friendly Meal Bowl

Serve one-fourth of the chickpea tofu with:

- At least half a plate of non-starchy vegetables, such as broccoli, kale, cabbage, bell peppers, mushrooms, or zucchini
- An intact whole grain or starchy vegetable, such as brown rice, quinoa, potatoes, or sweet potatoes
- Lemon juice, vinegar, salsa, fresh herbs, or the roasted red-pepper sauce

To keep the meal low in fat, avoid frying the tofu in oil or pairing it with coconut-based sauces, tahini, large amounts of avocado, or nut-based dressings.

### Calculation notes

- **Flour basis:** Four 30 g label servings supply 480 kcal, 84 g carbohydrate, 20 g fiber, 20 g protein, and 8 g fat to the batch. The lemon and measured spices add approximately 28 kcal and 7 g carbohydrate.
- **Measurement matters:** A cup is a volume measure, and flour weight varies with brand and packing. This is a 120 g calculation example, not a universal cup-to-gram conversion. Weigh your flour and use your package label for a more accurate estimate. The earlier 90 kcal estimate had no documented flour-weight basis.
- **Salt:** The salted estimate assumes approximately 1.5 g table salt in the whole batch. Crystal size and brand can change the weight of ¼ teaspoon. Water mineral content is not included.
- **Portions:** Divide the finished batch into four equal portions by weight. Cooking and crisping change water content, so a reliable per-100-g value requires weighing the finished tofu.
- **Sauce and sides:** Log these separately. Oil-free crisping adds no calories unless additional ingredients are used.
- These are calculated estimates from rounded label and reference values, not laboratory results. Whole-batch and per-serving numbers are rounded independently.

## Storage

- Refrigerate in a sealed container for 3–4 days at 40°F (4°C) or below.
- Separate layers with parchment paper to prevent sticking.
- For the best texture, crisp only the portion being served.
- Freezing is not recommended because it may produce a watery or crumbly texture after thawing.

## Personal Glucose Note

Because chickpea tofu contains carbohydrate, record the portion and observe the premeal, one-hour, and two-hour CGM response when eating it for the first time. Anyone using insulin or glucose-lowering medication should avoid changing medication doses solely on the basis of this recipe and should follow an individualized clinical plan.
