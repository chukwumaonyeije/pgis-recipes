# Oil-Free Chickpea Tofu

### Mastering Diabetes-Friendly

Firm, sliceable Burmese-inspired tofu made with chickpea flour, lemon, and spices.

**Serves 4** · One 8-inch pan  
Prep: 10 minutes · Cook: 10 minutes  
Cool: 20 minutes · Chill: 1–2 hours

---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chickpea flour
- 3 cups water, divided into two 1½-cup portions
- 1 teaspoon each onion powder and garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon each smoked paprika and turmeric
- ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon salt, optional
- 1 pinch cayenne or Nigerian red pepper, optional
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice

---

## Equipment and Setup

- Medium saucepan and whisk
- Heat-resistant spoon or silicone spatula
- 8-inch square pan or loaf pan
- Parchment paper

Line the pan with parchment. No oil is needed.

Whisk the chickpea flour with **1½ cups cold water** until completely smooth.

---

## Stovetop Cooking

1. Bring the remaining **1½ cups water** to a gentle boil with the onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, turmeric, pepper, and optional salt and cayenne.
2. Slowly pour in the chickpea-flour mixture, whisking continuously.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir continuously for **7–10 minutes**, until very thick and glossy and pulling away from the pan.
4. Stir in **1 tablespoon lemon juice**.

---

## Cooling and Setting

1. Immediately transfer the hot mixture to the lined pan.
2. Smooth the surface with a wet spatula.
3. Cool for approximately **20 minutes**, then refrigerate.
4. Chill for **1–2 hours**, or until completely firm.
5. Lift out using the parchment and cut into cubes, strips, or slabs.

Allow about **1 hour 40 minutes to 2 hours 40 minutes** before optional crisping.

---

## Oil-Free Crisping Options

**Air fryer:** 400°F (205°C) for 15–18 minutes. Turn halfway through.

**Oven:** 425°F (220°C) for 25–30 minutes on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Turn once.

**Stovetop:** Brown in a good nonstick skillet. Add small splashes of water or vegetable broth if needed to prevent sticking.

Arrange pieces in one layer. Timing varies with piece size and appliance. Crisp only the portion you plan to serve.

---

## Roasted Red-Pepper Sauce

- 1 roasted red bell pepper
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove
- Smoked paprika, black pepper, and cayenne to taste

Blend until smooth. Serve chilled or gently warmed.

Choose a pepper roasted without oil to keep the sauce oil-free.

---

## Meal Bowl

Serve **one-fourth of the tofu** with:

- At least half a plate of non-starchy vegetables, such as broccoli, kale, cabbage, mushrooms, or zucchini
- Brown rice, quinoa, potatoes, or sweet potatoes
- Lemon juice, vinegar, salsa, fresh herbs, or the red-pepper sauce

For the low-fat version in the supplied recipe, use these toppings in place of oil, coconut sauces, or rich nut-based dressings.

---

---

## Storage

- Refrigerate in a sealed container for **3–4 days at 40°F (4°C) or below**.
- Separate layers with parchment to prevent sticking.
- Refrigerate promptly after the brief cooling step.
- Crisp portions just before serving for the best texture.
- Freezing may leave the tofu watery or crumbly after thawing.

[USDA storage guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)
