Why Purple Sweet Potato?

Why Purple Sweet Potato?

"Purple sweet potato's GI (~44–50) rivals the carrot baseline — but adds a second weapon: anthocyanins that independently modulate postprandial glucose. This is PGIS ingredient selection at its most deliberate."
  • Anthocyanins — inhibit α-glucosidase, directly slowing glucose absorption
  • Resistant starch — partial undercooking preserves RS3, lowering net carb availability
  • Fiber density — ~4g per cup mashed, blunts postprandial spike synergistically
  • Natural sweetness — reduces date paste requirement vs. carrot base
  • Anti-inflammatory — polyphenol load supports recovery from training stress
  • No refined sugar — date paste only (fiber-buffered fructose)

Ingredients · Dry Base
Dry Base

Dry Base + Optional Add-ins

  • 1 cup rolled oats — blended to flour (30–45 sec pulse)
  • ¾ cup chickpea flour — protein anchor, low GI
  • 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1.5 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp cardamom — enhances PSP's natural sweetness
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg — warming note; pairs with purple sweet potato
  • ¼ tsp fine sea salt

Optional Add-ins

  • 2 tbsp walnuts — omega-3, fat-buffering for glucose curve
  • 1 tbsp hemp seeds — complete protein boost
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds — zinc + magnesium for recovery
  • ¼ cup blueberries — rest day only; additional anthocyanins
Chickpea flour note
If unavailable, substitute oat flour — protein and GI benefits are reduced. Soy flour is a closer nutritional substitute.

Execution Protocol
Execution Protocol

Method — 8 Steps

1
Steam PSP
Steam purple sweet potato 20–25 min. Cool slightly. Mash smooth — do not over-thin. Slight undercooling preserves resistant starch.
2
Flax eggs
2 tbsp flaxseed + 6 tbsp water. Rest 5 min until gel forms. Do not skip rest time.
3
Date paste
Blend 2 medjool dates + 2 tbsp warm water until completely smooth. Less required than carrot base.
4
Oat flour
Pulse rolled oats 30–45 sec. Do not over-blend. Coarser grind = more beta-glucan intact.
5
Combine dry
Whisk all dry ingredients together in large bowl until uniform.
6
Combine wet
Whisk soy milk, flax eggs, date paste, mashed PSP, vanilla, ACV until smooth.
7
Fold + rest
Fold wet into dry. Do not overmix. Rest batter 3–5 min — batter thickens as oats absorb moisture.
8
Cook + batch
½ cup batter per waffle, 5–6 min. Do not lift lid. Fridge 4 days · Freezer 6 weeks · Toaster reheat.

PGIS Notes
PGIS Notes

Practitioner Notes

  • PSP sourcing: Okinawan (Stokes) variety preferred — deep purple flesh = highest anthocyanin density. Starchy white interior = lower polyphenol yield.
  • Resistant starch tip: Steam, not boil. Cool to room temp before mashing. This maximizes RS3 formation and lowers effective GI.
  • Color retention: ACV in batter stabilizes anthocyanin pigment. Waffles stay purple-violet even after cooking.
  • No oil needed: PSP moisture + soy milk produce a dense, moist crumb without added fat. More forgiving batter than carrot base.
  • CGM context: ~18–28 mg/dL rise vs. ~50–80 mg/dL conventional. Lower ceiling than carrot base due to anthocyanin-mediated absorption inhibition.
  • Batch priority: Freezes exceptionally well. Purple color intensifies slightly after freeze-thaw cycle — a feature, not a bug.
