PGIS Rationale

Why Carrots?

"Carrots are a misunderstood PGIS ingredient. Their GI (35–47) is lower than most grains — and when paired with oat fiber and chickpea protein, the glucose curve stays remarkably flat."
  • Beta-carotene — Vitamin A precursor, anti-inflammatory during heavy training blocks
  • Insoluble fiber — slows digestion, blunts postprandial glucose spike
  • Natural sugars + fiber — sustained energy, not a spike
  • High water content — lighter texture without added oil
  • No refined sugar — date paste only (fiber-buffered fructose)

Ingredients · Wet Base

Wet Base

Yields 6–8 waffles · batch prep for 3–4 days

1.5 cups grated carrot — about 3 medium carrots, packed
1 cup unsweetened oat milk — or soy milk for more protein
2 flax eggs — 2 tbsp ground flaxseed + 6 tbsp water, rested 5 min
2 tbsp date paste — 3 medjool dates + 2 tbsp warm water, blended
1 tsp vanilla extract — pure, not artificial
1 tsp apple cider vinegar — activates leavening, no residual taste

Ingredients · Dry Base

Dry Base + Optional Add-ins

Dry Base
1 cup rolled oats — blended to flour
¾ cup chickpea flour — protein anchor, low GI
2 tbsp ground flaxseed
1.5 tsp baking powder
½ tsp baking soda
1 tsp cinnamon
½ tsp ground ginger
¼ tsp turmeric — anti-inflammatory
¼ tsp fine sea salt
Optional Add-ins
2 tbsp walnuts — omega-3, fat-buffering
1 tbsp hemp seeds — complete protein boost
¼ cup raisins — sweeter profile, use on rest days only
Chickpea flour note
If unavailable, sub oat flour — but protein and GI benefits are reduced.

Execution Protocol

Method — 8 Steps

1
Flax eggs — 2 tbsp flaxseed + 6 tbsp water. Rest 5 min until gel forms.
2
Date paste — blend 3 medjool dates + 2 tbsp warm water until smooth.
3
Oat flour — pulse rolled oats 30–45 sec. Do not over-blend.
4
Combine dry — whisk all dry ingredients together in large bowl.
5
Combine wet — whisk oat milk, flax eggs, date paste, vanilla, ACV.
6
Fold together — wet into dry, do not overmix. Fold in carrot. Rest 3–5 min.
7
Cook — ½ cup batter per waffle, 5–6 min. Do not lift lid early.
8
Batch & store — fridge 4 days · freezer 6 weeks · reheat in toaster.

PGIS Notes

Practitioner Notes

  • Carrot grating: Fine grate = invisible in waffle. Medium grate = visible texture, more moisture. Both work.
  • No oil needed: Carrot moisture + oat milk produce satisfying texture without added fat.
  • Batch priority: Holds post-freeze better than most WFPB waffles. Ideal for clinic weeks.
  • CGM context: ~20–30 mg/dL rise vs. ~50–80 mg/dL conventional. Fat pairing further dampens.
  • Chickpea sub: Replace with oat flour if needed — protein and GI benefits are reduced.
