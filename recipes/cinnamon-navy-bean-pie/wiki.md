# Cinnamon Navy Bean Pie

A lightly sweet, cinnamon-nutmeg navy bean pie with a creamy filling and an oil-free oat crust. Very ripe banana sweetens the filling, and navy beans supply most of the protein.

**Makes 8 slices · 20 minutes prep · 50–62 minutes baking · 1 hour cooling plus at least 3 hours chilling**

## Ingredients

### Oat crust

- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats (80 g)
- ⅓ cup unsweetened applesauce (80 g)
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon

### Navy bean filling

- 3 cups fully cooked navy beans (about 550 g), drained well (rinse if canned)
- 2 very ripe bananas (200 g total, peeled)
- ⅓ cup old-fashioned rolled oats (25 g)
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ¼ cup water (60 mL), plus up to 2 tablespoons as needed

## Instructions

1. **Preheat.** Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line the bottom of a 9-inch metal pie plate with a round of parchment.
2. **Make and par-bake the crust.** Pulse the crust oats into a coarse flour. Mix with the applesauce and cinnamon until evenly moistened. Press into a thin, even layer across the bottom and about ½ inch up the sides. Bake 12 minutes, until the surface looks dry.
3. **Blend the filling.** Blend the beans, bananas, filling oats, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and ¼ cup water until very smooth. Stop and scrape the sides as needed. If the blades stall, add water 1 tablespoon at a time, up to 2 tablespoons. The filling should be thick but spreadable.
4. **Bake.** Spread the filling evenly over the warm crust. Bake 38–50 minutes, until the edges are firm and the center jiggles slightly as one piece when you nudge the pan. The center should have no liquid ripple.
5. **Cool and chill.** Cool on a rack for about 1 hour, then cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight. Cut into 8 equal wedges. Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container for up to 4 days.

## Estimated Nutrition and Portions

About **175 calories, 34 g total carbohydrate, 9 g fiber, 8 g protein, and 1.5 g fat** per slice (⅛ of the pie, before toppings). Estimates use USDA reference values for the ingredients. Canned bean brands and drained weights can change the numbers.

Navy beans and bananas are on the Mastering Diabetes green-light list. This pie gets its sweetness from whole fruit and uses oats for the crust and to thicken the filling. It contains no added oil or sweetener.

Use total carbohydrate per slice when planning a portion. For smaller servings, cut 16 equal slices for about 17 g total carbohydrate per slice.
