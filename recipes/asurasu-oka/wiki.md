# Vegan, Oil-Free Asurasu Oka

Asurasu Oka is a savory Igbo steamed corn pudding. This adaptation keeps the fresh corn, onion, and Scotch bonnet, replaces ground crayfish with dried mushroom powder for savory depth, and omits the palm oil. Steaming gives the pudding its tender texture.

**Makes 4 individual puddings.** Prep about 15 minutes, steam 60–90 minutes, then rest for 10 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups fresh yellow corn kernels, cut from about 4–6 ears; measure the kernels because ear size varies
- 1 small onion, roughly chopped
- ¼ Scotch bonnet pepper for gentle heat, or more to taste; start with less if heat-sensitive
- 1–2 teaspoons finely ground dried shiitake mushrooms, to taste
- ¼ teaspoon salt, optional
- Up to ¼ cup finely ground whole-grain cornmeal, as needed to thicken
- Warm water, a tablespoon at a time, only if needed for blending

## Equipment

- Food processor or blender
- Four ungreased, 8-ounce heatproof ramekins or small steel pudding pots
- Foil or individual lids
- Large covered pot and a steaming rack that keeps the cups above the water

## Method

1. **Blend the corn.** Cut the kernels into a bowl, then scrape the cobs with the back of a knife to collect the milky pulp. Pulse the kernels, pulp, onion, and Scotch bonnet until mostly smooth, with a little corn texture remaining. Add warm water one tablespoon at a time only if the blades struggle.
2. **Adjust the batter.** Stir in the mushroom powder and optional salt. A spoonful should form a soft mound that slowly settles. If the batter is loose, stir in whole-grain cornmeal one tablespoon at a time, up to ¼ cup for the batch. Let it hydrate for 5 minutes before deciding whether to add more.
3. **Steam.** Divide the batter evenly among four ungreased, 8-ounce heatproof cups, leaving room below the rims. Cover each with foil or a lid. Set them on a rack above simmering water in a covered pot. Steam for 60–90 minutes, checking the water level occasionally and replenishing with hot water as needed. Keep the water below the cups and do not let the pot boil dry. Open the pot lid away from your face.
4. **Check the center.** Start checking at 60 minutes. The pudding is ready when the center feels set and a skewer comes out without wet batter. If still wet, cover and continue steaming, then check again. Rest for 10 minutes and serve warm by the spoonful, directly from the cups.

## Meal Bowl

Pair one pudding with black-eyed peas and steamed ugu or spinach. Spoon an oil-free tomato–pepper sauce over the greens. This lets the sweetness of the corn and the heat of the Scotch bonnet come through, while the peas and greens add fiber and protein.

## Adaptation Notes

- **Vegan savory flavor:** Finely ground dried shiitake replaces the ground crayfish described in the supplied source note. Start with 1 teaspoon and use up to 2 teaspoons to taste.
- **Oil-free texture:** No palm oil or greasing is needed. Serve by the spoonful rather than relying on unmolding.
- **Batter consistency:** Fresh corn varies in moisture. Use minimal blending water and add cornmeal only when needed.
