# Cinnamon Sweet Potato<br>Crunch Cereal

### Vegan · Oil-Free · No Added Sugar

Crisp oat clusters with gentle banana sweetness.

**Makes 6 servings**  
Prep: 10 minutes with a baked sweet potato  
Bake: 45–60 minutes · Cool: 20 minutes

---

## The Secret to the Crunch

Baked sweet potato **holds the oats together**. Half a ripe banana gives the clusters a gentle sweetness. Both fit the Mastering Diabetes approach.

- Bake the mixture in a **thin sheet** to set the structure.
- Break it into **small pieces** so the centers can dry.
- Let the clusters **cool completely** to finish crisping.

No butter, oil, or syrup needed.

---

## Ingredients · 6 Servings

- **2¼ cups old-fashioned rolled oats** (180 g)
- **⅔ cup mashed baked orange sweet potato** (about 150 g)
- **½ very ripe medium banana** (about 60 g peeled)
- **1 teaspoon ground cinnamon**
- **1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract**

Start with an already-baked sweet potato for the 10-minute prep time.

---

## 1–2 · Heat the Oven and Mix

1. Heat the oven to **325°F**. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with **parchment paper**.
2. In a large bowl, mash the sweet potato and banana with the cinnamon and vanilla **until smooth**.
3. Fold in the oats, pressing them into the mash with the **back of a spoon**.

**Texture check:** a spoonful should hold together when squeezed.

---

## 3 · Bake the Sheet

1. Press the mixture evenly onto the parchment, about **¼ inch thick**.
2. Bake at **325°F for 25 minutes**.
3. The surface should feel **set**, and the edges should begin to brown.

An even, thin sheet helps the mixture dry consistently.

---

## 4 · Form the Clusters

1. Break the baked sheet into **dime-sized pieces** with a spatula.
2. **Turn them over** and spread them apart.
3. Lower the oven to **300°F**.
4. Bake for another **20–35 minutes**, stirring halfway through.

Small pieces and space between clusters help the centers dry.

---

## Check the Center

Before taking out the batch:

1. Cool one of the **larger pieces** for a couple of minutes.
2. **Break it open** to check the center.
3. If the center is still soft, bake for **another 5 minutes** and check again.

Use the cooled-piece check to judge crunch throughout the cluster.

---

## 5 · Cool and Serve

Let the clusters **cool completely on the pan**, about **20 minutes**, so they crisp up.

- Serve with **fresh blueberries** and a splash of **unsweetened soy milk**.
- Or scatter a handful over **overnight oats** just before eating.

Milk, fruit, and overnight oats are separate from the cereal nutrition estimate.

---

## Estimated Nutrition and Portions

**Per serving: ⅙ of the batch, before milk or fruit.**

| Calories | Total carbohydrate | Fiber | Protein | Fat |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| About 145 | 28 g | 4 g | 4 g | 2 g |
