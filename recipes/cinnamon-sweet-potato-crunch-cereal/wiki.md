# Cinnamon Sweet Potato Crunch Cereal

Baked sweet potato holds the oats together, and half a ripe banana gives the clusters a gentle sweetness. Both fit the Mastering Diabetes approach. The trick to getting a good crunch without butter, oil, or syrup is to bake the mixture in a thin sheet, then break it into small pieces so the centers can dry.

**Makes 6 servings · 10 minutes prep with a baked sweet potato · 45–60 minutes baking · 20 minutes cooling**

## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups old-fashioned rolled oats (180 g)
- ⅔ cup mashed baked orange sweet potato (about 150 g)
- ½ very ripe medium banana (about 60 g peeled)
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven to 325°F.** Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. **Mix the cereal.** In a large bowl, mash the sweet potato and banana with the cinnamon and vanilla until smooth. Fold in the oats, pressing them into the mash with the back of a spoon. A spoonful should hold together when squeezed.
3. **Bake the sheet.** Press the mixture evenly onto the parchment, about ¼ inch thick. Bake for 25 minutes, until the surface feels set and the edges begin to brown.
4. **Form the clusters.** Break the sheet into dime-sized pieces with a spatula. Turn them over and spread them apart. Lower the oven to 300°F and bake for another 20–35 minutes, stirring halfway through. To check for doneness, cool one of the larger pieces for a couple of minutes and break it open. If the center is still soft, bake for another 5 minutes and check again.
5. **Cool and serve.** Let the clusters cool completely on the pan so they crisp up. Serve with fresh blueberries and a splash of unsweetened soy milk, or scatter a handful over your overnight oats just before eating.
